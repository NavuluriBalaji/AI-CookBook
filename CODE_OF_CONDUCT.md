# 📜 Code of Conduct

This project follows a **Code of Conduct** to ensure a welcoming and inclusive environment.  

---

## 🌟 Our Pledge  

We, as contributors and maintainers, pledge to:  
- Respect all participants, regardless of background  
- Encourage open, welcoming, and constructive discussions  
- Maintain a harassment-free environment  

---

## 🤝 Our Standards  

Examples of positive behavior include:  
- Being respectful and inclusive  
- Providing constructive feedback  
- Being open to new ideas and perspectives  

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:  
- Discrimination, harassment, or offensive comments  
- Personal attacks or trolling  
- Publishing private information without consent  

---

## ⚖️ Enforcement  

- Project maintainers may remove or edit contributions not aligned with this Code of Conduct.  
- Repeated violations may result in banning from contributions.  

---

## 🙌 Scope  

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and in public spaces where you represent the project.  

---

## 📬 Contact  

If you experience unacceptable behavior, please report it by opening an issue or contacting the maintainer directly.  

---

💙 Let’s build **AI Recipe Genius** together in a supportive, respectful, and fun way!
